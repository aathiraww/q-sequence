01 PLATFORM

Sequencing intelligence,
not just sequencing output.

Q-SEQ AMR helps researchers interpret sequencing runs in terms of biological readiness. Instead of only reporting raw sequencing statistics, it answers a sharper question — is the evidence you have generated sufficient for the conclusion you actually need to draw?

EVIDENCE ≠ DATA VOLUME

Every run is evaluated against the biological question it was started for — not against generic throughput thresholds.

02 THE PROBLEM

Coverage is not
the same as confidence.

Modern sequencing produces extraordinary volumes of data. Yet at the bench, the essential question stays unanswered: do I have enough evidence for the biological question I actually care about?

Run metrics describe machines, not conclusions. Reads, bases and mean depth say nothing about whether a specific resistance gene is resolvable, a species call is safe, or a critical locus is still starved of evidence. Q-SEQ AMR was designed to close that gap.

FIG. 01 — A RUN CAN PASS ON AVERAGE AND FAIL WHERE IT MATTERS.

  • P·01

    Over-sequencing

    Flow cells run for hours after the answer was already in hand — burning reagents, machine time and budget.

  • P·02

    Under-sequencing

    Runs are stopped on instinct or schedule, leaving key resistance loci below the evidence threshold.

  • P·03

    Uneven evidence across targets

    Global averages hide local starvation. One well-covered region can mask a critically under-covered gene.

  • P·04

    Uncertainty in AMR detection

    Absence of a resistance call is only meaningful if the evidence was sufficient to detect it in the first place.

  • P·05

    No principled stopping point

    Without a readiness signal, “when to stop” is a guess dressed up as a protocol.

  • P·06

    Data without direction

    Translating read generation into actionable biological readiness is left entirely to the researcher.

03 WORKFLOW

How Q-SEQ works

Four movements — from question to defensible decision.

  1. 01

    DEFINE

    Define the mission

    Researchers declare what the run must deliver — the species question, the AMR panel, the loci that cannot be left uncertain. The mission becomes the yardstick for everything that follows.

  2. 02

    MONITOR

    Monitor evidence in real time

    As reads stream off the sequencer, the platform maps every fragment against the mission — tracking not throughput, but accumulating biological evidence, target by target.

  3. 03

    EVALUATE

    Evaluate biological readiness

    Q-SEQ scores whether the run is sufficient for species identification, AMR gene detection and coverage of key loci — converting raw signal into calibrated readiness.

  4. 04

    DECIDE

    Decide the next action

    Continue, stop, review or resequence — a recommendation grounded in evidence quality, made at the moment it saves the most time and delivers the most certainty.
